About

A Business Analyst & AI Enthusiast

With 14+ years across telecoms, startups, and the NZ market, I don't just document requirements; I drive outcomes that stick.

I started on the front lines at Grameenphone (Telenor Group), managing customer systems serving 100,000+ daily interactions. The problems were real: fragmented tools, rising incidents, unhappy agents. I saw what broken processes cost not just in money, but in customer trust.

So I moved from monitoring to fixing. Over seven years as a Business System Analyst and Project Specialist, I led the CRM 360° transformation that replaced 9 legacy tools with one unified platform, cutting agent process steps by 60%. I drove a self-service IVR redesign that lifted adoption from 55% to 80% across 80 million customers. I reduced system incidents by 50% in two years not by luck, but by mapping root causes, aligning vendors, and closing gaps nobody else had bothered to close.

Now based in Auckland, I've completed my MBA at Massey University with Merit Distinction, and currently work at WeSmile, redesigning digital platforms through a user-first lens. Passionate about AI and agentic workflows, I enjoy turning complex challenges into practical outcomes. If you're looking for someone who connects strategy with execution, let’s discuss.
